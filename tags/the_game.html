The Game

The Game Headwear traces its origins to 1960 as a sports equipment company serving minor leagues and school teams, but it was the founding of The Game Headwear as a licensed brand in 1986 — built by fans, for fans — that set the company on its defining path. The mission was simple: make the best hat for someone who actually cared about their team. What followed was a run through the 1990s that put The Game at the center of college sports culture in America. Over time, The Game became the maker of the best-selling collegiate hat in the country, and the Retro Circle snapback became its signature. Bold team colors, clean circle logo graphics, and a curved brim that felt broken in right out of the box — these were the hats you saw in the student section, on the sideline, and everywhere in between. For an entire generation of college football and basketball fans, a Game snapback wasn't merchandise. It was the hat.

The Game has since merged with MV Sport, a leading supplier of apparel and accessories, expanding its operations across Alabama and New York. It remains an officially licensed collegiate headwear provider to this day. But it's the vintage pieces — the 90s circle logos, the faded twills, the deadstock finds — that keep collectors coming back. The Game earned its reputation one campus at a time, and those hats are the proof.
