Top of the World (TOW)

Founded in 1986 and based in Norman, Oklahoma, Top of the World built itself into the nation's leading collegiate headwear supplier, eventually holding licenses with over 550 colleges and universities across the country. While other brands chased professional sports, TOW went deep on college football and basketball at exactly the right time — the late 1980s and 1990s were when collegiate licensed merchandise exploded, and Top of the World was positioned right at the center of it. TOW participates with the NCAA as the official locker room headwear supplier for major championship events, a distinction that puts them in the same conversation as the brands that built the licensed headwear industry. The vintage TOW snapbacks from the early 90s carry that same energy — team colors, bold logos, and the kind of construction that held up through a decade of game days. For collectors, a deadstock TOW cap from that era is a direct window into what college sports looked like before everything got corporatized and consolidated.
