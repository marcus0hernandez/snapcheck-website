Welcome to Snap Check

The goal of this site is simple: to answer questions and preserve information about vintage snapbacks. There is a lot of knowledge scattered across collectors, forums, and old catalogs. By bringing that information together in one place, we hope to make it easier for anyone to learn about the hats they own or want to collect.
